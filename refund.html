Refund Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with Nexovias. This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund for purchases made through our website.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your subscription, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase. To request a refund, contact us at support@nexovias.com with your order details.

2. Subscription Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month. You can cancel at any time to prevent the next renewal. Once a renewal has been charged, that billing period is generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

3. Custom & Project-Based Work

Custom builds, implementation fees, and one-time project work are non-refundable once development has begun, as these involve dedicated engineering time. Specific terms for project work are set out in your individual order or proposal.

4. How Refunds Are Processed

Payments are processed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Paddle and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider. You may also contact Paddle directly through your purchase receipt for billing assistance.

5. How to Request a Refund

  • Email support@nexovias.com with the email address used at checkout and your order or receipt number.
  • Tell us the reason for your request (optional but helpful).
  • We aim to respond to all refund requests within 3 business days.

6. Contact

For any questions about this Refund Policy, contact support@nexovias.com.
